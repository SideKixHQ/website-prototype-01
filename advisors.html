Your time
You do not need to be available all the time, just when it works for you.

As a business owner your time is valuable. As a SideKix Advisor you choose how much of it you spend with users, with no minimum required. Every day, once a week, once a month. It is entirely up to you.

This is not a full time job. It is a flexible way to give back, grow your influence and earn on your own terms.

Advisor FAQs

The questions people ask first.

What is the time commitment?

This is up to each Advisor, based on how active they wish to be on the SideKix app. A minimum of one to two hours per week works well, and some Advisors choose to do far more, taking on daily meetings, working closer with entrepreneurs, or offering various services.

Are Advisors employees?

No. Advisors are independent contractors.

How are Advisors paid?

Up to the Advisor. You determine your services and what to charge. If you are looking to give back to the community pro bono, you can set available office hours and provide advice and expertise without charging any fees. It is entirely up to you, and SideKix is here with assistance or suggestions if you want them.

Do I need an LLC?

Not mandatory. Payment can be made directly to an LLC if one is established. All tax, insurance and liability responsibilities remain with the Advisor, so although an LLC is not required on day one, some Advisors choose to form one.

Messaging or video?

Both. A survey indicated that 62% of respondents preferred messaging support, so sessions start there, and the Advisor can move a session to video at any point.
