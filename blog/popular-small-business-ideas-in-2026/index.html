Popular Small Business Ideas in 2026

Twenty-one small business directions for 2026, grouped by field — tech, health and wellness, sustainability, and a few others worth a look.

Published 8 July 2026 Reading time 2 minutes Category News

Thinking of owning and running a small business but not sure what kind of business you want it to be? Want to know what your options are for something with strong earning potential?

Here are some of the top business ideas in 2026 that will help you figure out what you’d like to do.

The Tech World

Services in the digital world will always be in high demand as tech is constantly evolving. We’re also more reliant on it now than ever before.

You don’t necessarily need a background in tech, but it might be worth learning some skills, as AI, cybersecurity, and cloud services are on the rise. Here are some popular tech business ideas this year:

  1. AI Consulting and Set up
  2. AI Powered Customer Support
  3. AI Literacy Service
  4. Cybersecurity Services
  5. Cloud Modernization Services
  6. Web Development and Design
  7. Software Development

Health & Wellness

This field isn’t as rigid as tech, since there are so many different kinds of small businesses that fit under the health and wellness umbrella. Here are some possible business directions you could go in:

  1. Health and Wellness Coaching Services
  2. Personal Training Services
  3. Mobile Services, like home healthcare for physical therapy and rehab, or in-home pet grooming
  4. Clean Beauty Products
  5. Meal Prep & Delivery

Eco-Friendly and Sustainability

With increasing attention on health, wellness, and the current state of our planet, we’re more conscious than ever about businesses being more eco-friendly.

It’s also a win-win for potential founders like yourself: you’ll earn a profit while also making a positive impact on the environment. Here’s what business owners with green thumbs are doing:

  1. Sustainable Clothing Boutique
  2. Thrift Stores
  3. Sustainable Landscaping
  4. Secondhand Item Rentals and Resale
  5. Reusable Packaging

Other Directions

None of these speaking to you? Here are a few other options:

  1. Creative Freelance Work
  2. Digital Marketing
  3. Online tutoring
  4. Dropshipping

Pick a Direction, Not Every Detail

While you don’t need every single facet figured out, you do need a solid direction to head in — and hopefully you found this list helpful.

Frequently asked questions

What are good small business ideas for 2026?

The article covers ideas that suit small teams and low starting costs, weighted toward services rather than inventory.

What is the easiest business to start with no money?

Service businesses built on skills you already have, because they require time rather than capital and can be tested with a single customer.

Are most new businesses solo?

Yes. Census data puts nonemployer businesses, meaning those with no paid employees, at 78.4% of all US establishments in 2023.

How many businesses actually get started each year?

Roughly 5.6 million business applications were filed in 2025. The market data page tracks this alongside survival rates.
