What to Do After Having Your Business Idea

You’ve just had an idea for a business– the lightbulb is glowing and you’re so inspired that you’re ready to get to work. But that is until you realize that you’re not sure how to start.

Published 12 June 2026 Reading time 3 minutes Category Members
You’ve just had an idea for a business– the lightbulb is glowing and you’re so inspired that you’re ready to get to work. But that is until you realize that you’re not sure how to start. If this sounds like you, then definitely keep reading! We’re going to show you the first steps to take to develop your idea and move it from your brain into reality.  Write Everything Down! In your notes app, type it up on your computer, or even a pen and paper– make sure you write down your idea, your vision, the problem you wish to solve with your idea, and aesthetics so you don’t forget anything! Don’t worry about it being a professional business plan the moment you start writing; have this be where you brainstorm and word vomit. Make sure to keep this handy as you’ll need it for reference in later steps and to add to it when you have more ideas. Answer These Starting Questions When creating a business, not matter what it is, these are questions you really need to consider: 
  1. Who are your target customers? 
  2. What is the problem they have that you are trying to solve? 
  3. Why is it important that this problem is solved?
  4. How does your business idea solve this problem?
  5. Why should someone patronize your business instead of finding an alternative?
This will help you further flesh out your idea and validate why your business idea should be a reality.  Research Before you can create a detailed business plan for your idea, you need to research your target customers, competition, the problem that your idea is supposed to solve, the costs for making this idea into a reality, and related legalities (like business structure, name registration, taxes, and making a business-only bank account). This information will help you plan the best ways to market, invest, finance, and establish your business. Gain Insight From Your Target Customers Find groups to talk to or check out online forums where those who are struggling with the problem you’re trying to solve are discussing it. This will allow you to gain a much deeper insight so you can make your solution a lot more considerate and help prove that there is an audience for you out there who would support your business.  Join Entrepreneur and Business Owner Communities You should also find groups related to entrepreneurship so you can get an idea of what it’s like and what it takes to build a business. By joining groups, forums, and communities, you gain valuable insight, can seek advice, and find resources. You can also network with business professionals, connect with other new entrepreneurs, and even find those who also want to solve the same problem to collaborate with.  Start Creating A Business Plan Now that you have all your ideas down, you’ve answered critical starting questions, and did your research, you have a good collection of information to begin forming a detailed business plan. This should include information you’ve gathered from completing the above, from business description to the execution.    We know that the list above is easier said than done and may seem like a lot of work ahead, but this is so you can take a great first step. It’s important to really plan and cover all your bases so you don’t go into this big responsibility blind.
