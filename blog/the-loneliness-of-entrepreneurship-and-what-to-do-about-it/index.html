The Loneliness of Entrepreneurship and What to Do About It

Explore the issue of entrepreneur loneliness and discover ways to connect with others for support and collaboration in your journey.

Published 5 June 2026 Reading time 2 minutes Category Community
One thing about entrepreneurship that isn’t talked about enough is the loneliness that can come with it. A lot of entrepreneurs don’t have anyone to share the burdens or at least someone in their lives who supports what they’re building. This makes the journey to success eventually lead to a burn out, as all the responsibilities fall on one person and encouragement to keep going comes from within. If this sounds like you, here’s what you can do about it.  Change the Scenery  Seek out a shared environment to work in rather than working in solitude. A local coworking space would be ideal, as it’s a workplace where business professionals and entrepreneurs of all different companies can work together. These spaces provide desks, private offices, conference rooms, lounges, and opportunities to collaborate with others all through a membership. Attend Networking Events Find events related to what you’re building or for business and entrepreneurship in general. This will give you ample opportunities to connect with other entrepreneurs and professionals. They may also give you an opportunity to display your business so you can put yourself out there.  Join A Community There are lots of forums and chat spaces online all about entrepreneurship, solopreneurs, and small businesses. Reddit, for example, has several online communities dedicated for entrepreneurs to network, get advice, find resources, and talk about their personal experiences.  Hire A Mentor Business coaches and mentors not only provide valuable insights, advice, and feedback but can provide a safe space to discuss your personal struggles in pursuing entrepreneurship.    We understand that all the suggestions above sound easier said than done, as making meaningful connections can take a lot of your effort and time. Not to mention putting yourself out there can be intimidating all on its own. But know that, while the chance of your business being the roaring success you want it to be is TBD, consistently reaching out to others will bring you the support system you were looking for.  At SideKix, we believe that no one should ever have to build alone.
