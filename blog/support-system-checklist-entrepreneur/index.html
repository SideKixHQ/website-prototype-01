Your Support System Audit

Personal Support

☐ Someone in my personal life who genuinely supports my entrepreneurial journey
☐ At least one person I can call when I'm having a hard day — no judgment, just support
☐ Clear boundaries with people who drain my energy or undermine my confidence
☐ Practices that protect my mental health (exercise, sleep, time off)

Peer Support

☐ At least 2-3 fellow entrepreneurs I'm in regular contact with
☐ An accountability partner (or I'm actively looking for one)
☐ A community or group I belong to (online or in-person) specifically for entrepreneurs
☐ Someone I can bounce ideas off who will give me honest feedback

Mentorship & Expertise

☐ A mentor or advisor I can turn to for strategic guidance
☐ Access to legal advice (even just knowing a lawyer I could call)
☐ A bookkeeper or accountant who understands my business
☐ Connections in my industry who keep me current

Resource Support

☐ At least 3 months of runway in savings or credit
☐ A clear understanding of what funding options exist for my business
☐ Access to free or low-cost business resources (SBDC, SCORE, library programs)
☐ Tools that automate or simplify my most time-consuming tasks

Community Support

☐ A local business community I'm part of
☐ An online community where I actively participate (not just lurk)
☐ A platform or organization like SideKix that connects me with like-minded entrepreneurs
☐ Relationships I'm investing in regularly — not just when I need something

Frequently asked questions

What is an entrepreneurial support system?

An entrepreneurial support system is the network of people, tools, and resources that help you sustain and grow your business. It includes mentors, peers, family, professional advisors, online communities, and personal wellbeing practices.

How do I build a support system as an entrepreneur?

Build a support system as an entrepreneur by proactively joining communities, finding a mentor, identifying 2–3 peers at your stage for peer support, communicating your needs clearly to family, and establishing professional relationships with accountants, lawyers, or coaches.

Why do entrepreneurs need a support system?

Entrepreneurs need a support system because the journey is isolating, high-stakes, and emotionally demanding. Without a support network, burnout is more likely, mistakes are harder to recover from, and the lack of accountability makes it easier to give up.
