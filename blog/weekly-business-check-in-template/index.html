Frequently asked questions

What should a weekly business review include?

A weekly business review should include: reviewing your goals and key metrics, celebrating wins, identifying blockers, planning your top 3 priorities for the coming week, and checking in on your energy and wellbeing as the business owner.

How often should entrepreneurs review their business?

Entrepreneurs should review their business weekly at a minimum — a quick 30-minute check-in covering metrics, priorities, and blockers. A deeper monthly review and a quarterly strategic review are also important for staying on track with bigger goals.

What is a business check-in template?

A business check-in template is a structured framework that helps entrepreneurs regularly review progress, assess challenges, and plan next steps. It typically includes prompts for wins, metrics, priorities, obstacles, and personal wellbeing to ensure balanced progress.
