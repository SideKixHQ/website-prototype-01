Frequently asked questions

Why do so many Americans want to be their own boss?

Research shows that 90% of Americans want to be their own boss at some point. The desire for autonomy, financial independence, and the ability to build something meaningful drives this aspiration. Most people want more control over their time, income, and future than a traditional job can offer.

Why don't more people actually start a business?

The most common barriers are fear of failure, uncertainty about where to start, and financial concerns. These are real challenges — but they are all solvable with the right tools, guidance, and community support. The gap between aspiration and action is where most people get stuck.

What is the first step to becoming your own boss?

The first step is identifying what you would offer — a skill, service, or product with real market demand. From there, validate it with at least one paying customer before investing significant time or money. The biggest mistake is over-planning and under-starting.
