Tennessee

Starting an LLC in Tennessee

What it costs, what you file and what you owe afterwards. Every figure below came from the state's own pages.

To form an LLC in Tennessee you file the Articles of Organization with the Tennessee Secretary of State, Division of Business Services. The current fee could not be confirmed from an official source, so it is not quoted here. After that, Annual Report is due periodically

What it costs

WhatAmountWhen
Articles of OrganizationNot confirmedCheck the state page before filing
Annual Report$300

Registered agent

Tennessee requires one. Every entity except a general partnership has to keep a registered agent and office in Tennessee at all times, and the registered office must be a real Tennessee street address rather than a post office or route box.

What you owe after year one

None

Missing it is the most common way a good standing lapses, and restoring one costs more than filing on time.

Worth knowing

The annual report fee is a floor, not a flat rate: $300 minimum, rising by $50 for each member above six, capped at $3,000. Most of the Secretary of State's fee and due-date pages refused automated retrieval during this check.
