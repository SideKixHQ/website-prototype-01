How it works

Back to SideKix

A complete business
operating system.

SideKix™ is for potential and existing entrepreneurs who don’t know where to begin or how to keep going. At its core, it’s a movement amplifying and connecting voices that are often unheard. SideKix exists for people who have ambition, ideas, and potential, but not always the support system, roadmap, or opportunity to move forward confidently.

SideKix gets to know you, draws your roadmap from what it learns, and provides everything you need at the exact moment you need it.

Scroll
01Kix listens first

Your guide starts by listening.

Kix opens with a conversation. An idea you never acted on, a business three years in, a career the market is rewriting. It starts where you are, draws your map from your words, and tells you the truth, even when it doesn’t know the answer.

03Inside a milestone

Each milestone opens into steps.

Tap one and it becomes steps you can start straight away, one at a time, so the next move is always clear and always small enough to finish.

05Community

You are not alone.

A live feed of people like you. Ask anything, learn from someone a week ahead of you, connect and share your wins, learns, and challenges as you build.

07Squads

Then a few people who are yours.

A squad is the close group you build with. Private and invite only, where you work through things together, share the numbers you would not post, and show up for each other week after week.

09Booking

One on One Support.

Accessible Advisor Sessions, booked when you need them. Say what you need, attach anything useful, and your Advisor reads it first, so the session starts on the problem.

11Discovery

Learn it, then put it to work.

Discoveries, resources and trainings in one place, suggested against the milestone you are on. Short enough to finish in one sitting, built to be used straight away, and everything you buy stays yours.

02The map draws itself

Your map draws itself.

Milestones in the order that makes sense for you, drawn straight from the conversation. It redraws whenever something changes, and the line between them lights as you move, so progress is something you can see.

04Intentional connections

Right thing, right time.

Advisors, resources, trainings written for your situation, and outside tools we have vetted. Each one arrives when it will help most, and tells you why it arrived. Ask it something yourself.

06Live rooms

Get a room.

Restauranteurs, Finance, Operations, whatever you are building. Ask something specific and get a specific answer. Moderated, so it stays a place to trade ideas and meet people like you.

08Advisors

Someone has stood exactly here.

Suggested from what you have in common, the actions you have taken, and where their expertise sits. People who have been in your shoes and can help you start, and keep going, at any stage and in any industry.

10The session

Talk it through.

Message or video, inside SideKix, with the notes you take written straight back into your journey. Clarity before you act, which is the whole point of asking someone.

12Self Discovery

Know yourself, then act on it.

Short discoveries measure the mindsets, behaviors and skills you are working with. You get a profile and a score across five dimensions, and your map uses both to shape actions you are far more likely to finish.
