Questions people ask

Do these tools cost anything?

No. There is no account, no email step and no payment. Everything runs in your browser.

Is anything I type saved or sent?

The calculators and generators keep nothing at all. The worksheets and checklists save your answers in your own browser so you can return to them, which means they stay on that device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I use these on a phone?

Yes. Every tool works on a phone, and the worksheets print and download from there as well.
