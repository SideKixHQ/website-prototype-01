1SCOPE

This Privacy Policy applies to SideKix websites, web applications and browser-based desktop experiences, mobile applications, Kix, Journey Maps, Discovery and Self Discovery, Community features, Advisor features, events, customer support, communications, and other SideKix services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Services").

Certain features may launch at different times. This Policy applies to a feature when that feature is made available.

2INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on how you use the Services, SideKix may collect:

  • Account and profile information, such as name, email address, username, authentication information, profile details, avatar or profile image, preferences, and account settings.
  • Kix and AI interaction information, such as prompts, messages, voice inputs, transcripts, responses, feedback, Journey Map information, goals, milestones, and related context.
  • Discovery and Self Discovery information, such as assessment responses, reflections, scores, preferences, learning activity, progress, and generated insights.
  • Community information, such as posts, comments, reactions, room participation, reports, messages, group or Squad participation, and other user-generated content.
  • Advisor and appointment information, such as booking details, communications, session information, notes where applicable, ratings, reviews, and transaction information.
  • Event information, such as registrations, attendance, event preferences, accessibility requests, event communications, and authorized recordings or photographs.
  • Transaction information, such as subscription status, purchases, Kix Credit activity, transaction identifiers, and limited payment-related information. Payment card details may be processed directly by payment providers rather than stored by SideKix.
  • Support and communications information, including support requests, surveys, feedback, emails, and other communications with SideKix.
  • Device, technical, and usage information, such as IP address, browser or device type, operating system, app version, device or app identifiers, session data, logs, crash information, diagnostics, security events, referral information, and interactions with the Services.
  • Information from permissions you choose to grant, such as microphone, camera, photos or files, notifications, contacts, or location, but only when relevant to an enabled feature and subject to applicable device controls.
  • Information from partners or third parties, where permitted, such as authentication providers, payment providers, Advisors, event partners, analytics providers, referral partners, or service providers.

5KIX, VOICE, TRANSCRIPTS AND AI PROCESSING

Kix and other AI features may process information you submit or make available through the Services in order to understand requests, generate responses, personalize experiences, build or update Journey Maps, recommend next steps, and provide related functionality.

Voice experiences may involve several distinct processing activities:

  • Microphone access. SideKix accesses the microphone only when permitted by the device and relevant to a voice feature.
  • Voice processing. Audio may be transmitted or processed to recognize speech, enable a conversation, detect turn-taking, or provide a response.
  • Transcription. Spoken content may be converted into text.
  • Conversation data. Transcripts, prompts, responses, summaries, memories, or related context may be processed to provide continuity and personalization where enabled.
  • Service providers. Authorized AI, speech, infrastructure, storage, safety, analytics, or other technology providers may process information on SideKix's behalf to provide these functions.

Granting microphone permission is not, by itself, consent to every possible secondary use of audio or transcripts. SideKix will provide additional notice or controls where required for materially different uses.

6AI AND PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT

SideKix may use information to evaluate, secure, debug, and improve its Services, subject to applicable law, contractual restrictions, and user notices or controls.

Where SideKix uses de-identified or aggregated information, it will take reasonable measures designed to prevent the information from being associated with an identifiable individual where required by law.

If SideKix offers a setting or consent choice concerning use of identifiable conversations, voice data, assessments, or other content for AI or model improvement, SideKix will honor that choice as described when the choice is presented.

SideKix does not sell user conversations to third parties for their independent external AI training.

7DEVICE PERMISSIONS

Certain features may request access to device capabilities or information. SideKix will request permissions in context and only when relevant to a feature.

Permissions may include:

  • Microphone, for Kix voice conversations, calls, or other audio features.
  • Camera, for video calls, profile or content capture, or other camera-enabled features.
  • Photos or files, when you choose to upload, save, select, or share media or documents.
  • Notifications, for reminders, messages, appointments, events, Journey activity, security alerts, and optional marketing where separately permitted.
  • Contacts, only if SideKix introduces a feature that requires or benefits from contact selection or invitations. SideKix uses minimum-scope system pickers where reasonably available instead of broad address-book access.
  • Location, only if SideKix introduces location-dependent functionality. SideKix requests the minimum level of location precision reasonably needed for the feature.

You can generally deny or later revoke device permissions through your operating-system settings. Some features may not function if a permission necessary for that feature is denied. SideKix will not require unrelated permissions merely to access unrelated functionality.

8PUSH NOTIFICATIONS AND COMMUNICATION PREFERENCES

If you allow notifications, SideKix may send functional notifications relating to the Services, such as account or security notices, Kix or Journey reminders, Advisor appointments, messages, Community activity, event updates, or other requested functionality.

SideKix may also offer promotional or marketing push notifications. Where required, SideKix will obtain the appropriate opt-in before sending promotional push notifications and will provide an in-app method to turn those notifications off.

As features become available, SideKix may provide notification preferences by category, such as:

  • Kix and Journey;
  • Advisors and appointments;
  • Community;
  • Events;
  • Account and Security; and
  • SideKix Updates and Offers.

System-level notification permission can also be changed through device settings. Certain important non-marketing communications may still be sent by email or other permitted channels when necessary to administer an account, transaction, security matter, or legal notice.

17ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

Users may request deletion of their SideKix account through the in-app account settings or another deletion method made available by SideKix.

SideKix will delete or de-identify personal information associated with a deleted account as required by applicable law and platform requirements, except information SideKix is permitted or required to retain for purposes such as legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, transaction records, or enforcement of agreements.

Deletion of an account is different from cancellation of a subscription. Users should review applicable subscription or app-store billing requirements before deleting an account.

SideKix will also maintain an external web method for requesting account deletion where required by applicable platform rules.

If deletion requires time to complete, SideKix may provide information about the expected process and any information that must be retained.
