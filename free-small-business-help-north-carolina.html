Free Small Business Help in North Carolina

Three statewide networks will sit down with a North Carolina business owner and work through their problem, at no charge, funded already by taxes you have paid. Most owners have never heard of any of them.

SideKix is based in Wilmington, so this is the list we would give a neighbor. Everything named here was checked against the organization's own material. Nothing on this page is sponsored and nothing pays to be included.

The three networks that cost nothing

The Small Business Center Network runs out of North Carolina's 58 community colleges. The system describes its counseling as completely free and confidential, with training that is free or low cost. This is the one to start with if you are early, because there is a center near almost everyone in the state and the seminars are aimed squarely at people who have not done this before. The statewide calendar sits at ncsbc.net and filters by distance from your zip code.

The SBTDC, the Small Business and Technology Development Center, is part of the UNC System and goes deeper. Business strategy, growth, government contracting and exporting. There is an office at UNC Wilmington, and centers across the state. Counseling is provided at no charge to North Carolina businesses.

SCORE pairs you with a volunteer mentor who has already run a business, at no cost, by email, phone or video. It is the most informal of the three and the easiest to start. SCORE Cape Fear Region covers the Wilmington area.

Using all three is normal. They are funded to be used and they are not competing for your business, because none of them are selling anything.

Wilmington and the Cape Fear region

Beyond the three networks, the local ecosystem is denser than most people realize.

  • Cape Fear Community College Small Business Center and Brunswick Community College Small Business Center, free confidential counseling and seminars, both listed through ncsbc.net.
  • UNCW Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, mentorship, resources and access to university talent.
  • 1 Million Cups Wilmington, weekly, where founders present to a room that wants them to succeed.
  • Network for Entrepreneurs in Wilmington, monthly educational events.
  • Genesis Block, an accelerator focused on minority owned and women owned businesses.
  • VentureSouth Wilmington and Wilmington Angels for Local Entrepreneurs, for companies raising early capital.

Capital, when a bank says no

Community development financial institutions exist for the gap between a viable business and a bank's checklist.

  • Carolina Small Business Development Fund, statewide lending and SBA loans.
  • Partner Community Capital and Carolina Community Impact, CDFI lending for borrowers who do not clear traditional criteria.
  • NC Rural Center, which administers state capital programs including SSBCI.
  • NC IDEA, seed grants and programming for early companies.
  • Live Oak Bank, headquartered in Wilmington and one of the largest SBA lenders in the country.

What to do first

If you have not started yet, book a session with the Small Business Center at your nearest community college and go to the next free seminar on their calendar. It costs an afternoon.

If you are running something already and the problem is strategic, the SBTDC is the better first call.

If what you want is somebody who has done it before to talk to, SCORE is the shortest path to that conversation.

None of these replace each other, and none of them will charge you.

Frequently asked questions

Is small business counseling really free in North Carolina?

Yes, for the three main networks. The NC Community College Small Business Center Network describes its counseling as completely free and its training as free or low cost. SCORE mentoring is provided at no cost through the SBA. The SBTDC provides in depth counseling to North Carolina businesses at no charge, funded through the SBA and the UNC System. None of them are trying to sell you anything afterwards.

What is the difference between the SBCN, the SBTDC and SCORE?

The Small Business Center Network runs out of the 58 community colleges and is strongest on getting started: seminars, workshops and first counseling sessions close to where you live. The SBTDC sits in the UNC System and goes deeper on strategy, growth, government contracting and exporting. SCORE pairs you with a volunteer mentor who has run a business, and is the most informal of the three. Using all three is normal and nobody minds.

Where do I find free business workshops near me in North Carolina?

The statewide calendar at ncsbc.net carries the seminars from every community college Small Business Center, and you can filter it by distance from your zip code. The SBTDC keeps its own calendar for the UNC System centers. Most sessions are either online or within a short drive, and most cost nothing.

What help is there specifically in Wilmington?

Cape Fear Community College and Brunswick Community College both run Small Business Centers with free confidential counseling. The SBTDC has an office at UNC Wilmington. SCORE Cape Fear Region provides free mentoring. Beyond counseling, 1 Million Cups Wilmington meets weekly, the Network for Entrepreneurs in Wilmington runs monthly events, and Genesis Block runs an accelerator focused on minority and women owned businesses.

Where can a North Carolina business get a loan if a bank says no?

Community development financial institutions exist for exactly that gap. Carolina Small Business Development Fund, Partner Community Capital and Carolina Community Impact all lend to businesses that do not clear a traditional bank's criteria. The NC Rural Center administers state capital programs, and NC IDEA provides seed grants to early companies. Live Oak Bank, headquartered in Wilmington, is one of the largest SBA lenders in the country.
