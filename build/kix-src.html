Questions

About Kix.

What is Kix?

Kix is your Entrepreneurial Support Guide within the SideKix app. It helps you plan, focus, and name the action you can take today, then puts the resource or the person who makes that action easier right in front of you.

Is Kix a coach?

No. Kix is a guide. Asked directly what it is, Kix says: “I’m Kix, an AI system inside SideKix.”

Can I talk to Kix, or do I have to type?

Either. You can speak or type, and Kix Credits measure the conversation the same way.

What does Kix cost?

350 Kix Credits are $19 and cover up to 90 minutes of conversation with Kix. There’s no subscription, and credits never expire. Your Milestones and Steps never use credits. Memberships start at $39 a month.

Which phones does Kix work on?

SideKix is on iPhone now. Android and the web are not available yet. Join the waitlist and you’ll be the first to know when your platform is live.

Is there a daily limit?

Up to 90 minutes of conversation with Kix a day. Your Milestones and Steps stay open all day regardless.

Kix’s answers

Every answer, in writing.

The same words Kix says above, for reading at your own pace.

I don’t have the money

Money’s usually the first wall people name, and it’s a real one. Often the better question is how little you need to find out if this works. What’s the smallest version you could sell to one person? Let’s price it out line by line tonight. If outside money makes sense after that, I’ll help you get ready before you ask for it, and show you where you’d actually fit.

I don’t have the time

Twenty minutes a night is enough to move something, as long as it’s the right twenty minutes. When could you protect three short blocks this week? Pick them now. I’ll hand you one step that fits each block, small enough to finish, and the plan keeps track so you don’t have to.

I don’t know where to start

You don’t need the whole plan to start. You need the next step, and finding it is my job. What’s the idea in one sentence, the way you’d tell a friend? Write that down today. From there we’ll lay out the first few steps together, in an order that fits your life, and I’ll bring the training for each one when you get there.

What if it doesn’t work?

That fear usually means this matters to you. So we aim to fail small, early and cheap, before anything big is riding on it. Which part are you most worried won’t work? Let’s test that part first, this week, with something you can try in an afternoon. Whatever happens, you’ll know more than you do now.

The doors aren’t open to me

A lot of advice assumes you already have the money, the contacts, or the right title. Plenty of people building something don’t, and they build anyway. Which door feels most closed right now: money, people, or know how? Tell me, and I’ll find the people, programs and training that open it, and help you get ready to walk through.

I’m doing this alone

Building alone is heavier than it looks from the outside, and you don’t have to carry all of it. Who’s one person you could tell about this today, even in a text? I’ll be here whenever you get stuck. And when you’re ready, I’ll connect you with people building something like yours, and advisors who’ve stood where you are.

It isn’t ready yet

Ready usually shows up after people see it, not before. What’s the smallest piece you could show one person this week? Pick a date, show it as it is, and let their reaction tell you what to fix first.

Everything feels urgent

When everything’s urgent, it’s hard to move anything. If only one thing moved this week, which would make the rest easier? Do that one first. I’ll hold the rest of the list so it isn’t sitting on you.

Something else

Whatever it is, say it the way it comes out. I’ll listen first, ask what I need to, and then we’ll work out the next step together.
