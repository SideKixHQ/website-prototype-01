Small Business Events in Puerto Rico

10 upcoming events for people starting, growing or leading a business in Puerto Rico. Hosted by SBDCs, colleges, nonprofits, banks and companies. Online, livestreamed and in person, with the cost shown as the host states it.

Checked at the source. Updated 2026-09-21.

Most of these are not advertised anywhere a founder would think to look. They sit on the calendars of organizations that do not compete for attention, so the events run half empty while the people who would benefit never hear about them. This page gathers what is scheduled in Puerto Rico over the next few months, in one place, refreshed weekly.
